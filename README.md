# Project Mamono Insects

An insect sub-mod of Project Mamono for RimWorld 1.6. It adds sixteen insect mamonos -
monster girls who keep the bug they came from - and two tribes of their own to live in.

- **Sixteen insect mamono castes.** Builders, diggers, farmers, weavers, soldiers and
  queens, each with her own body, her own three colours and her own work.
- **Two insector tribes.** A neutral hive that trades and can be befriended, and a swarm
  that is always at war; both live in villages grown from hive walls and creep.
- **The abaddon queen and her brood.** She lays the daughter you choose instead of eggs,
  and her swarm fights for her.
- **Something from every caste.** Silk from an arachne, honey from a bee, chitin from the
  butcher's block, and a greenworm who spins a cocoon and comes out a papillon.

**Requires:** Harmony, Biotech, Project Mamono, Big and Small - Framework, Vanilla Expanded Framework, Vanilla Races Expanded - Insector, Vanilla Factions Expanded - Insectoids 2

**Optional:** Ideology (the work groups and memes the villages use), Alpha Animals (the web an arachne throws), Medieval Overhaul (its own silk and honey in place of ours)

## Content

Sixteen insect mamono live in the world, in villages of hive walls and creep. You meet
them as raiders at your door, as caravans that trade, as villagers you attack, and as
women you take in - and each caste works at something of her own.

### Genes

The six genes the mod adds. Every caste also carries genes from Project Mamono and from
Vanilla Races Expanded - Insector.

- `insectoid` - chitin under the skin and feelers that read the air. Toxic environment
  resistance +100%, metabolic efficiency -1. Insect meat counts as human meat to her, so
  eating it is cannibalism.
- `honey` - she fills with honey as she works the plants and tends the animals, and it
  can be milked out of her once there is enough. Metabolic efficiency -2, complexity 2.
- `beetle armament` - her carapace grows one of three killing tools when she is born:
  ripper-blades, mandibles or a megaspider horn. Complexity 1.
- `quarrelsome` - -50 opinion of every other woman carrying this same temper, and it
  runs both ways. Metabolic efficiency +1, complexity 1.
- `honey-hating` - -50 opinion of anyone carrying the honey gene, one way only: the
  honey bee feels nothing back. Metabolic efficiency +1, complexity 1.
- `disorientating lights` - +10 mood and -5% consciousness while she stands in a
  man-made light. The bright core of a growing lamp is the one light that does nothing
  for her. No metabolic cost.

### Events

The mod adds no incidents of its own. Everything happens through the two tribes, on
vanilla's own systems.

- **Raids** - the insector swarm is a permanent enemy, so it raids on the storyteller's
  own rules and never stops. Its raiders are melee only: neither tribe has a ranged
  group.
- **Village attacks** - a village map holds its people, hive walls, creep and hives. The
  queen stands in a swarm village's garrison as the rarest woman there, and she never
  joins a raid, so a village you attack is the only place to meet her. Both tribes are
  led by an abaddon queen.
- **Trade** - the neutral hive sends caravans once your colony is worth visiting, and a
  player caravan that reaches one of its villages can trade there. A giant ant does the
  trading, and the mothman and the papillon lead most of the caravans.
- **Prisoners** - a raid that downs one of your men can end with him bonded, and then
  carried off to a village as a prisoner. A rescue can free him from there. This is the
  core mod's rule for every mamono, and the two tribes follow it like the rest.

### Abilities

- **fly** - "Take wing and fly to a distant location, soaring over anything in
  between." Every caste carrying a flight gene has it, and it asks for no line of
  sight, so she clears walls and water. Cooldown 1 second, warmup 0.5 seconds, range
  29.9 cells, no cost.
- **web trap** (arachne) - "Throw a web of sticky silk that stuns whoever it hits."
  Cooldown 10 seconds, warmup 1.5 seconds, range 12 cells. The web itself is Alpha
  Animals' work: without that mod she has no web trap.
- **egg spew** (abaddon) - "Lay an egg sac on the ground. It opens by itself a day later, and
  breaking it before then kills the brood inside." Cooldown 15 days, warmup 0.25 seconds, range just under 2 cells. The
  same button is her brood order: pick which of the other fifteen castes she lays, and
  leave it unset for a random one.
- **Milk honey** (honey bee) - a right click on her by any of your pawns, or the button
  on her own bar. A full honey bar is worth 12 jars, and the button stays greyed out
  until the bar is three quarters full.
- **Gather silk** (arachne, ant arachne) - a right click on her by any of your pawns, or
  her own hands through her work type. A full gather is 25 silk, about three days of
  spinning.

### Factions

- **Insector hive** - the neutral tribe, and the one you can deal with. It starts at
  peace, sends caravans once your colony is worth visiting, lets you trade at its
  villages, and raids only if relations turn bad. Devil bugs, giant ants, soldier
  beetles, ant arachne and honey bees stand in its villages, the hornet is its fighter,
  and the mothman and the papillon lead its caravans. An abaddon queen leads the tribe.
- **Insector swarm** - the hostile tribe, and a permanent enemy: it raids, it never
  deals, and its raiders carry melee weapons only. Devil bugs, giant ants, soldier
  beetles, vamp mosquitoes and mantises fill its raids, while its villages hold the
  weavers, the beelzebub, the girtablilu and the queen. An abaddon queen leads this
  tribe too.
